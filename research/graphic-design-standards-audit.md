## Translation to the marketing skill
1. Interpret brief before design; distinguish new design, edit, critique and prompt request.
2. Ask only about consequential missing facts; otherwise use safe, reversible defaults.
3. Treat approved design elements as protected across revisions.
4. Use hierarchy, legibility, composition, contrast, imagery and format awareness without a mandatory house style.
5. Verify output and report uncertainty; image-generation prompts do not guarantee exact copy or dimensions.
6. Do not import third-party scripts, assets, fonts, service dependencies or source text wholesale.
