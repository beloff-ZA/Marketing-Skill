# Marketing foundations (V1 provisional)

## Durable concepts
- Audience relevance: connect the message to real needs, situations and motivations; do not invent audience research.
- Positioning: communicate why an offer matters and how it differs, using verified differentiators.
- Distinctive assets: preserve existing brand cues and avoid unnecessary identity changes.
- Mental and physical availability: useful research lenses, not automatic prescriptions for every task.
- Long and short term effects: distinguish brand building from immediate activation; do not assert a universal budget split.
- Measurement: choose metrics linked to stated goals; do not mistake impressions for business outcomes or correlation for causation.
- Creative clarity: message, hierarchy, readability, relevance, platform context.
- Ethical practice: avoid deceptive claims, manufactured scarcity, fabricated reviews, and unsupported performance promises.

## Evidence protocol
For each nontrivial empirical claim, record source, date, population/context, method, limitations and applicability. Award wins and case-study results are not automatically causal proof. Never turn a creative example into a general law.

## Sources to validate and expand
