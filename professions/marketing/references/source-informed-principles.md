# Source-informed marketing principles (V1.2 research foundation)

Load only for requests requiring marketing planning, research, creative evaluation, or professional explanation. Sources and page locators: `research/uploaded-reference-audit.md`.

## Strategy is not the same as tactics
The Moderandi 2013 guide separates strategy, enabling tools and customer acquisition activities (PDF pp. 2, 4-5). Treat this as a scope-control mechanism:
- A caption or poster request is a tactical/creative deliverable.
- A brand identity request concerns tools/assets.
- A campaign or go-to-market strategy request permits strategic analysis.
Do not promote a request to a higher level without authorisation.

## Customer, offer and context
Kotler and Armstrong (2018) present marketing as customer-value creation and exchange, supported by research and strategic planning. For strategy tasks, establish the offer and available customer evidence before proposing targeting or positioning. Avoid invented persona demographics or claims of product-market fit.

## Strategic questions are conditional
Fifield (2008) structures strategy around business drivers, environment, customer, segmentation/targeting, positioning/branding, retention, organisation, offerings and implementation. Diasz (2017) covers audits, competitors, stakeholders and planning. Use these as optional prompts only when relevant; never require users to answer 100 questions or complete a SWOT before a small task.

## Campaign execution
The university advertising-campaign text includes campaign objectives, creative strategies, content, media, budgets and evaluation (MSM-531(C), PDF p. 2 contents). Apply the chain *objective -> message -> appropriate channel -> measurement* only when campaign planning is in scope. Do not invent budgets or forecast outcomes.

## Communications and feedback
The South African N6 lecturer guide includes a sender/message/channel/receiver/feedback model (PDF p. 6), and distinguishes advertising, PR, personal selling, sales promotion and direct marketing. Consider message clarity, channel suitability, noise and feedback in communication reviews. Its historical descriptions of advertising oversight are NOT a statement of current South African law.

## Stakeholders and implementation
The ELSA 2013/14 handbook includes internal/external communication, stakeholder objectives and practical research (PDF pp. 3-4). Useful for charities, associations, schools and member organisations; do not apply ELSA-specific structures to unrelated businesses.

## Models are tools, not laws
STP, marketing mix, SWOT, AIDA and other frameworks can structure thinking but are not universally appropriate, sufficient, or empirically proven guarantees. Explain the selected lens only when it adds value. Attribute distinctive named frameworks (e.g. Fifield's SCORPIO) and do not reproduce protected diagrams.

## Evidence and currency
Separate textbook concepts, case-specific observations, independently verified research, professional judgement and hypotheses. Old sources do not validate current ad-platform features, regulations, algorithms, privacy requirements, conversion benchmarks or ROI expectations. Verify those separately when requested.

## Artifact boundaries
Page-aware documents and proportional spreadsheets are governed by the core modules. Marketing knowledge does not authorise automatic branding, decorative letterheads, complex analytics or unrelated marketing deliverables.
