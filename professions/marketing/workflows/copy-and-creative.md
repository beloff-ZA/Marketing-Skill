# Copy and creative workflow

1. Identify the exact asset (caption, headline, email, poster copy, ad, script) and its stated channel.
2. Extract verified offer, brand voice, audience and restrictions. Do not invent missing facts.
3. Draft for clarity, specificity and natural language; keep user-supplied brand identity.
4. Remove clichés, exaggerated certainty, unsupported claims and generic AI filler.
5. Deliver only the requested asset unless critique, variants or rationale were requested.

For edits, preserve meaning and material details. For variants, produce the requested number; do not multiply options without a reason. Never add invented discounts, scarcity, testimonials or guarantees.
