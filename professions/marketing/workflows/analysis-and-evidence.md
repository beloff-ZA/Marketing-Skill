# Review, research and measurement

Review: evaluate against the user's actual goal and known audience, not arbitrary taste. Describe what works, what fails, why, and the smallest effective correction.

Research: favour original sources, authoritative studies and published methodology. For each claim, distinguish verified fact, company/agency-reported outcome, inference and hypothesis. Cite accessible sources. Do not fabricate campaign metrics or infer causal uplift from anecdotes.

Measurement: define the metric, period, denominator, attribution caveats and data source. If data is missing, identify what is needed rather than generating a plausible number. For personal account data, use authorised access or user-provided exports only.
