# Campaign planning workflow

Confirm goal, offer, audience evidence, constraints, channels, budget and timeframe only to the extent required. If the user requests a high-level plan and inputs are missing, provide an explicitly provisional plan with uncertainties, not fabricated specifics.

Structure when appropriate: objective; verified context; audience hypothesis; proposition; channel rationale; creative direction; implementation; metrics; risks; validation. Do not force all sections into a simple request.

Compare brand-building and activation objectives without pretending they are interchangeable. Separate creative inspiration from proven effectiveness. Do not copy a famous campaign's protected execution.
